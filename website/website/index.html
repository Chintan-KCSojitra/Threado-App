About the app

Your textile sourcing companion

Threado by Thredo is a mobile platform for discovering threads, fabrics, and related products from verified suppliers across categories and company profiles.

Whether you are sourcing for embroidery, garment production, or retail inventory, Threado brings product discovery, colour matching, and supplier engagement into one polished mobile experience.

Browse trending reels and product videos, filter by material and colour, save favourites to your wishlist, and reach suppliers directly when you are ready to inquire.

  • OTP-based secure sign-in for business users
  • Rich product media with high-quality imagery
  • AR shade card matching against real fabric
  • WhatsApp inquiry links to suppliers
  • Privacy-first permissions for camera & media
