## 1. App Review Notes (Copy/Paste)

**App Purpose & Target Audience:**
Threado is a curated B2B textile marketplace for traders, manufacturers, and garment exporters. Unlike open platforms, **all company profiles and product data are manually verified and uploaded by the Threado internal team** to ensure catalog accuracy and supplier legitimacy. The app facilitates thread discovery, AI-assisted color matching, and direct communication between buyers and verified suppliers.

**Authentication & Demo Access:**

- **Note**: This application does not currently use OTP-based authentication. The demo credentials provided will grant immediate access to all features.

**External Services:**
- **Primary API**: Custom backend (https://api.threadoapp.com) for product catalogs and company profiles.
- **Maps**: Uses Google Maps via external URL for supplier location discovery.
- **WhatsApp**: Integrated for direct B2B inquiry flows between buyers and sellers.

**Regional Differences:**
The application is currently focused on the Indian textile market. While login defaults to the +91 country code for convenience, all marketplace features are functional globally.

**Account Deletion:**
Users can delete their account directly within the app via **Profile > Delete Account**. This triggers a permanent deletion of the user's profile data from our servers.

**UGC Policy:**
Users can submit company reviews. We maintain a report/block mechanism for all User Generated Content to ensure community safety.

## 2. Physical Device Recording Script

Perform this recording on a **physical iPhone** (not a simulator).

1. **Start**: Launch app (Show Splash animation).
2. **Setup**: Choose "English" on the language selection screen.
3. **Login**: Enter the demo credentials (Phone/Password) and accept Terms.
4. **Dashboard**: Scroll through the Home grid and tap a featured banner.
5. **Product**: Open a Product Detail page, swipe through images, and view the specification sheet.
6. **AI Match**: Open the "Match" tab (FAB) and take a photo of a fabric sample.
7. **Suppliers**: Open the "Company" tab, select a supplier, and scroll through their products/reviews.
8. **Settings**: Open the "Profile" tab, show the Wishlist, and open the "Delete Account" dialog.
9. **End**: Logout of the application.
